# TP GROWTH MKT — LOCKED SYSTEM DESIGN DNA

This document defines the "Locked" system design for the TP Growth MKT brand. All future components, pages, and sections MUST strictly adhere to these rules to maintain the premium strategic operator positioning.

---

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY
**"Calm Clarity over Flashy AI"**
- **Trust (Blue):** Use for systems, systems thinking, logic, and primary branding.
- **Action (Orange):** Use EXCLUSIVELY for Call-to-Actions (CTA), highlights, and active indicators.
- **Premium (White/Light):** Use generous whitespace and light backgrounds to create an editorial, high-end business feel.

---

## 2. COLOR PALETTE
- **Primary Blue (Trust):** `#2563EB`
- **Primary Orange (Action/CTA):** `#EA580C`
- **Background:** `#F8FAFC`
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text (Primary):** `#0F172A`
- **Text (Secondary/Body):** `#475569`
- **Border:** `#E2E8F0`

---

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY
- **Headings:** `Sora` (Weight: 700, tracking: -0.03em)
- **Body:** `Inter` (Weight: 400/500/600)
- **Hierarchy:** Maintain clear editorial rhythm. Large H1s, readable body line-height (1.7).

---

## 4. COMPONENTS RULES
- **Buttons (CTA):** Orange background, Bold text, `12px` radius.
- **Cards:** White surface, `20px` radius, `shadow-brand-soft` (Subtle 0 10px 30px rgba(15,23,42,0.06)).
- **Navigation:** Transparent or subtle white, absolute fixed or sticky with backdrop blur.

---

## 5. VISUAL RULES
- **Avoid:** Neon glow, cyberpunk, crypto-style, heavy gradients, or cluttered UI.
- **Prioritize:** Spacing, alignment, and strategic data visualization (clean charts/funnels).
